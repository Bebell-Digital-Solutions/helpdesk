# HelpDesk

> Nunca pierdas otro cliente por responder tarde.
> Unified customer communication and operations platform that centralises messaging channels into a single inbox.

Developed by: Bebell Digital Solutions
Website: https://helpdesk.icu/
Target Audience: Clinics, Restaurants, Retail/eCommerce, and Service Offices.

---

## Core Value Proposition
HelpDesk unifies communication from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, Email, and more into a single collaborative inbox, allowing teams to respond faster, automate workflows, and boost sales without changing their existing WhatsApp numbers.

---

## Key Capabilities & Features

### 1. Multichannel Unified Inbox
Centralise customer conversations across 12 distinct communication vectors:
- **WhatsApp Business:** Official integration via WhatsApp Business API (supports multiple simultaneous agents on the same number).
- **Instagram DM:** Conversational tool designed for product discovery and building relationships.
- **Facebook Messenger:** High-engagement platform with over 1 billion active users.
- **Live Chat (Web):** Full live chat widget to embed on websites to capture and engage visitors in real-time.
- **SMS (via Twilio):** High open-rate offline notifications.
- **Telegram:** Speed and privacy-focused communication.
- **Viber:** Multimedia-rich messaging, widely used in Europe and Asia.
- **LINE:** Highly popular business integration channel in Japan and Southeast Asia.
- **WeChat:** Crucial ecosystem for Chinese consumer support and messaging.
- **Zalo:** Vietnamese leading messaging app with 79 million active users.
- **Email:** Manage support emails in the same interface without tab switching.
- **API Channel:** Unifies custom external software or third-party applications.

### 2. No-Code Visual Chat Automations
- Drag-and-drop conversational flow builder.
- Visual workflow configuration (e.g., Welcome Message -> Real-time Notification -> AI Agent Assignment -> Ticket Creation -> Sync to Google Sheets).
- Automated ticket distribution, priority settings (urgency/skill/channel), and intelligent routing.

### 3. AI-Powered Support Agents
- Integrate smart, AI-driven assistants directly into conversational flows to resolve queries and handle front-line support automatically.

### 4. Advanced Lead Capture & Utilities
- **Custom Forms:** Gather structured data using conditional, dynamic fields before starting a conversation to eliminate repetitive questions.
- **Knowledge Base:** 24/7 self-service help center to empower customers and reduce support friction.
- **Web Widgets / Private Chat:** Live widgets designed to prevent cart abandonment and retain site visitors.

### 5. Performance Analytics
- Track average agent response times (targeting under 2 minutes).
- Detailed message volumes broken down per communication channel.
- Performance metrics tracked per individual agent.
